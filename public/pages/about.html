About FaaUnicode

FaaUnicode is a professional Unicode Explorer built with pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Browse thousands of Unicode characters, search instantly, copy in multiple formats, inspect codepoints, analyze text, and generate decorated nicknames.

Everything works completely offline after the initial load.
